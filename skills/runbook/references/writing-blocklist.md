# Runbook writing blocklist

Apply this complete list to the finished runbook's prose regions. Do not apply
it inside code fences, command output, URLs, identifiers, or citations.

## Words and phrases

- Do not use an em dash.
- Do not use `leverage` as a verb, `utilize`, `empower`, `unlock`,
  `revolutionize`, `game-changing`, or `transformative`.
- Do not use `at the end of the day`, `circle back`, `deep dive`,
  `let's dive in`, or performative hedges such as `arguably`, `one might say`,
  and `it could be argued`.
- Do not use `showcase` as a verb, vague `robust`, `actually`, or `just`.
- Do not add a sports metaphor as decoration.
- Do not use `it's worth noting`, `importantly`, `delve`, `foster`, `synergy`,
  `underscore`, `pivotal`, `paradigm shift`, `game changer`, `spoiler alert`,
  `in today's fast-paced world`, or `full stop`.
- Do not use a `Question? Answer.` heading pattern or the contrast pattern
  `This isn't about X. It's about Y.`

Prefer plain replacements. Use `use` instead of `leverage` or `utilize`, state
the claim instead of announcing its importance, and address the operator
directly instead of using a generic third-person reader.
